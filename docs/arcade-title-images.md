# Arcade title images

Updated 9 October 2026. The splash and library images are 1920 × 1080 JPEGs captured from the published apps in a 960 × 540 browser viewport at twice the pixel density.

- `img/stormwatch-title.jpg`: [Stormwatch](https://jaxsbr.github.io/stormwatch/), using its main title artwork and original wordmark. Settings and profile controls were hidden for the capture; the title was enlarged and centered vertically.
- `img/tidekin-title.jpg`: [Tidekin](https://jaxsbr.github.io/tidekin/), using its main menu artwork and original wordmark. Menu controls were hidden for the capture; the title was enlarged.
- `img/little-light-library-title.jpg`: [Little Light Library](https://jesusfilm.github.io/little-light-library/), using the library's actual illustrated book covers. The carousel was brought closer, all covers were shown at full brightness, and the library heading was enlarged. Navigation controls were hidden for the capture.

- `img/reef-hop-title.webp`: the approved [Clear turquoise reef reference](https://github.com/Jaxsbr/reef-hop-prototype/blob/main/assets/references/reef-hop-clear-turquoise.png), exported as WebP at the original 1672 × 941 dimensions with the full composition preserved.

The capture adjustments were temporary browser styles. The original apps and their artwork were unchanged. No artwork was regenerated.
